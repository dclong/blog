- `apt-get` (and `apt-*`): low-level (with deps management)
- `apt`: user friendly
- `aptitude`: even more user friendly
- ~~wajig~~: use `apt` or `aptitude` instead

Overall, 
`apt` and `aptitude` are recommended.
